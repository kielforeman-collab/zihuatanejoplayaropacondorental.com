# Casa OM del Sur — Playa La Ropa Condo Rental

Casa OM del Sur is a luxury 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom vacation rental condo in the Punta Marina complex at Playa La Ropa, Zihuatanejo, Mexico, overlooking the bay.

## The condo

- 3 king bedrooms, each with a private ensuite bathroom and rain shower
- Fully equipped galley kitchen: gas stove, air fryer, fridge, cookware, tableware
- Huge covered patio overlooking the bay, with private BBQ and three sitting areas
- Shared swimming pool with chaises in the Punta Marina complex
- All bed linens, bath towels, and beach towels included
- High-speed WiFi; water, gas, and electricity included
- Sleeps up to 6 guests

## Location

#103 Punta Marina, Playa La Ropa, Zihuatanejo, Guerrero, Mexico. A 10-minute walk down the hill (including 74 steps) to the sandy beach of Playa La Ropa. The complex has overnight security staff and a gated entrance.

## Rates

$1,000–$2,500 USD per week, depending on season, duration, and occupancy. Minimum stay one week. High Season December–April; Low Season May–November. A $350/week cleaning fee, a $1,000 refundable damage deposit, and a $2,000 CAD reservation deposit apply. See [Rates](https://www.zihuatanejoplayaropacondorental.com/rates.md) for details.
