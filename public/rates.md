# Rates & Pricing — Casa OM del Sur

## Weekly rental rates

$1,000–$2,500 USD per week. Weekly vacation rental prices vary depending on the season, duration, and occupancy. Ask for exact pricing for your dates.

- Minimum stay: one week
- High Season: December–April
- Low Season: May–November
- Sleeps up to 6 guests comfortably

Additional charges: $350/week cleaning fee, $1,000 refundable damage deposit, $2,000 CAD reservation deposit.

## What's included in the rate

- All linens, bath towels, and beach towels
- Access to the complex swimming pool and chaises
- Fully equipped galley kitchen and air fryer
- Water, gas, and electricity utilities
- Complex maintenance and night security guard
- High-speed WiFi internet access
