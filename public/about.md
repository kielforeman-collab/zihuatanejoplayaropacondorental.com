# About Playa La Ropa — Zihuatanejo, Mexico

Playa La Ropa is one of the most beautiful sandy beaches along Mexico's Pacific coast. Its gentle waves, warm waters, and scenic bay views make it a paradise for families, swimmers, and outdoor enthusiasts.

Depending on the season, you can witness pelicans diving for fish and local sea turtle releases. Activities on the beach include paddle boarding, Hobie Cat rentals, parasailing, jet-skiing, water rides for kids, snorkeling, and sailing.

## Beach information

- Beach type: fine white sand, very swimmable
- Distance from the condo: about a 10-minute walk down the hill
- Water temperature: broadly warm year-round
- Activities: snorkeling, sailing, paddle boarding, beach dining

## Local dining recommendations

- [Paty's Marymar](https://patys-marymar.com/): Beachfront al fresco dining, full bar, table service, wheelchair accessible. The host's favourite casual eatery and bar on the beach — smoothies, breakfasts, lunches, casual evening dining, and yoga in the upper salon most days.
- [La Gaviota](https://www.tripadvisor.ca/Restaurant_Review-g150795-d1032486-Reviews-La_Gaviota-Zihuatanejo_Zihuatanejo_de_Azueta_Pacific_Coast.html): Right on the beach, ideal for sunset dinners. Outside seating, full bar, credit cards accepted, free WiFi, family and dog friendly.
- [Jungle Pizza](https://www.tripadvisor.ca/Restaurant_Review-g150795-d1379281-Reviews-Jungle_Pizza-Zihuatanejo_Zihuatanejo_de_Azueta_Pacific_Coast.html): Just back from the beach across from the OXXO store. Delivery, takeout, outdoor seating, full bar. The host calls it the best pizza in town.
- [Trattoria da Gianni](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g150795-d7747587-Reviews-Trattoria_da_Gianni_Zihuatanejo-Zihuatanejo_Zihuatanejo_de_Azueta_Pacific_Coast.html): Italian bistro on the access road just behind Playa La Ropa. Indoor and garden seating, pasta and wine. Also on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/trattoriadagiannizihuatanejo).
