# Frequently Asked Questions — Casa OM del Sur

## How much does it cost to rent Casa OM del Sur for a week?

Weekly rates run $1,000–$2,500 USD, depending on the season: High Season is December–April and Low Season is May–November. The exact price depends on your dates and occupancy — contact the host for a quote.

## What is the minimum stay?

One week. Casa OM del Sur is a weekly vacation rental, so nightly stays are not available.

## How many guests can the condo sleep?

Up to 6 guests, in three king bedrooms.

## What is included in the weekly rate?

All bed linens, bath towels, and beach towels; access to the complex swimming pool and chaises; a fully equipped galley kitchen with air fryer; water, gas, and electricity; complex maintenance and a night security guard; and high-speed WiFi.

## Are there additional fees?

Yes: a $350 cleaning fee per week, a $1,000 refundable damage deposit, and a $2,000 CAD reservation deposit.

## How do I book Casa OM del Sur?

There is no online booking system. Contact host Oscar Mario Sanchez through the inquiry form, by email at info@puntamarinazihuatanejo.com, or by phone or WhatsApp at +52 755 103 6035. Reservations are finalized once your deposit is received.

## What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation, refund, and maximum-guest details are provided with your custom quote — ask the host when you inquire.

## How far is the condo from Playa La Ropa beach?

About a 10-minute walk down the hill, including 74 steps, to the sandy beach.

## What are the bedrooms and bathrooms like?

Three king bedrooms, each with its own private ensuite bathroom with a rain shower.

## When can we check in and out?

Check-in and check-out times are flexible by arrangement.
